Analysis

The WHO recommends at least 150 minutes of moderate activity per week for adults, yet many people have no simple way to see how their habits compare. Numbers such as steps, sleep and water are easy to log but hard to interpret.

Analysis. Rule-based statistics make trends visible and reproducible. A language model then explains them in friendly words, which improves understanding without replacing the numbers.

Conclusion. DenimSau combines both approaches with clear safety limits: it informs and motivates, it never diagnoses.

⚠️ Educational project. Data is simulated or typed by you. AI output is informational and not a substitute for professional medical advice.
