Welcome to Almaty

Almaty is the largest city in Kazakhstan and one of its main student hubs, home to universities such as KBTU, KIMEP, Al-Farabi KazNU and SDU. Surrounded by the Tian Shan mountains, the city offers a mix of cozy coffee shops, parks, affordable food, coworking spaces and entertainment venues that make student life comfortable and interesting. This guide collects some of the best places for students to eat, study, relax and have fun in Almaty.

Where to Eat

Navat Restaurant

A large restaurant serving traditional Kazakh and Uzbek dishes such as beshbarmak and plov, popular for group dinners.

Address: Al-Farabi Ave 7, Almaty

View on Google Maps

Bao Wow

A budget-friendly Asian street food spot known for steamed bao buns, popular among students for quick lunches.

Address: Zhibek Zholy St 65, Almaty

View on Google Maps

Line Brew

A casual restaurant and brewery with European and local dishes, good for group hangouts after classes.

Address: Dostyk Ave 89, Almaty

What to Do

A hill with a cable car ride and a great panoramic view over Almaty, with cafes and an amusement area on top.

Address: Kok-Tobe, Almaty
