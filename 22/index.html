About DenSaulyq

DenSaulyq is an IoT healthcare system that connects wearable and bedside sensors to track the vitals that matter most for metabolic syndrome — temperature, glucose, heart rate, blood pressure and heart rhythm — and shows every reading on one screen for patients and their care team.
